# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive and safe for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience, identity or role in the school community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and professional.
- Give actionable technical feedback, not personal criticism.
- Assume good intent while still addressing concrete problems.
- Protect student, parent and staff privacy.
- Respect the project's essentials-only scope and security boundaries.
- Credit other people's work and follow the project's licensing terms.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private student, parent or staff information.
- Sharing credentials, tokens or other secrets.
- Deliberately disrupting the live service or other contributors.
- Pressuring maintainers to weaken security or access controls.
- Using the project to harm, monitor or exploit members of the school community.

## Privacy and student data

This project handles school records. Never post real names, contact details, grades, fees, discipline records or admission documents in issues, pull requests, screenshots or discussions. Use anonymized examples.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports should be made privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Do not include sensitive personal data in the report.
